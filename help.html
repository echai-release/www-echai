Quick Answers

  • What is LoopX and who is it built for?

    LoopX is a revenue intelligence system built for sales managers, VPs, and CROs who need to validate deal reality during pipeline and forecast reviews.

  • Does LoopX replace my CRM?

    No. CRM records activity. LoopX evaluates meaning from internal and external signals to surface what's real and what's hope.

  • How do I get started with LoopX?

    LoopX is available now. Visit the LoopX page to try it or book a 30-min demo with our team.

  • How do I join the Whispr beta?

    Whispr is in beta. Visit the Whispr page and click "Join Beta" to book a call with our team.
